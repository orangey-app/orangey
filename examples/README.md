# Examples

Ordinary `.orangey.json` files. Copy them into your library folder, drag one
onto the app, or open the import wizard and choose one.

- `dnd/` — encounter and treasure wheels, and the three dice expressions that
  come up most in play (`d20 + 5`, `2d20kh1`, `4d6kh3`).
- `boardgames/` — a first-player wheel to rename for your group.
- `generic/` — a yes/no oracle, shown as a list rather than a wheel.

`dnd/forest-encounters.csv` is the same wheel as a spreadsheet export, to try
the import wizard with.
